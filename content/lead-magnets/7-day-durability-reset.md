## Day 1 — Anchor your sleep

**Subject:** Day 1: the one lever that moves everything
**Preview:** Fix this and the other six days get easier.

Welcome to the Durability Reset.

For the next seven days, I'm going to give you one small, high-leverage action each morning. Not a life overhaul — one lever. Do it, and by Day 7 you'll feel the difference a recovery-first approach makes.

We start where every durable athlete starts: **sleep.**

Here's the truth most people miss — you don't get fitter in your workout. You get fitter *recovering* from it. And the deepest recovery you get all day happens while you sleep.

**Today's action:** Pick a **fixed wake time** and set it for all seven days — weekends included. Same time every morning.

That's it. A steady wake time is the anchor your entire body clock hangs on. Nail the wake-up and bedtime, energy, and hunger all start falling into line on their own.

## Day 2 — Nasal-breathing baseline

**Subject:** Day 2: you're breathing wrong (most people are)
**Preview:** A free upgrade you can make every minute of the day.

How you breathe when you're *not* thinking about it shapes your stress, your sleep, and even your endurance.

The default should be simple: **in and out through your nose.** Mouth breathing keeps you slightly stuck in "fight or flight." Nasal breathing improves your CO₂ tolerance, calms the system, and — over time — makes hard efforts feel easier.

**Today's action:** For the whole day, breathe through your nose — walking, working, warming up. If you catch yourself mouth-breathing, just close it and reset. Notice how much calmer you feel by evening.

Keep yesterday's fixed wake time going. Tomorrow: the highest-return nutrition habit there is.

— Coach

## Day 3 — Hit your protein target

**Subject:** Day 3: the only nutrition number that matters this week
**Preview:** Forget the diet. Do this instead.

We're not counting every calorie this week. We're fixing the one nutrient that drives recovery, strength, and staying lean: **protein.**

Most people fall short — and it quietly caps everything the training could give them.

**Today's action:** Aim for a solid dose of protein (a palm-sized portion or more) at **every meal.** A rough daily target for most training athletes is around 0.7–1 g per pound of bodyweight. Don't stress the math today — just make protein the thing you build each meal around.

*(General guidance, not a prescription — adjust to your own needs.)*

Wake time steady? Nose breathing? Good. Tomorrow we move — to recover, not to grind.

— Coach

## Day 4 — Move to recover

**Subject:** Day 4: the movement that pays you back today
**Preview:** 10 minutes. Same-day return.

Recovery isn't just lying still. The right *easy* movement pumps blood, calms the nervous system, and leaves you feeling better than when you started — same day.

**Today's action:** Take **10 minutes** for easy movement. A relaxed walk, some gentle mobility for your hips, ankles and shoulders, a few easy rounds of breathing on the floor. Keep it low — this should feel restorative, not like a workout.

This is what "recovery as training" means: you program the easy work with the same intent as the hard work.

Tomorrow: how to tell whether today is a push day or a back-off day.

— Coach

## Day 5 — Manage the load

**Subject:** Day 5: push or back off? Here's how to know
**Preview:** The 30-second check that prevents most injuries.

Elite performers aren't the ones who go hard every day. They're the ones who go hard *on the right days* — and recover on the rest.

Your body tells you which is which. You just have to check.

**Today's action:** Each morning, run a quick **readiness check** — three questions:
1. How did I sleep the last night or two?
2. How's my energy right now?
3. How sore/beat-up am I?

**Mostly good?** Green light — train hard. **Mostly rough?** Back off ~20%, or make today an easy/recovery day. No guilt — that's the smart play, not the soft one.

Two days left. Tomorrow: the routine that deepens your sleep quality.

— Coach

## Day 6 — Wind-down protocol

**Subject:** Day 6: win the last hour, win your sleep
**Preview:** Better sleep is built before you get in bed.

You anchored your wake time on Day 1. Today we protect the *quality* of the sleep itself — and that's won in the last hour of the day.

**Today's action:** Build a simple **wind-down** for the 30–60 minutes before bed:
- Dim the lights and get screens out of your face
- A few minutes of slow breathing — **longer exhale than inhale** — to downshift
- Keep the room cool and dark

You're telling your nervous system the day is done. Do it a few nights running and you'll fall asleep faster and wake up more recovered.

Tomorrow — the last day — we turn this week into something that lasts.
